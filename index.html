The model

A formal supply chain for materials that used to end in landfill.

Every kilogram moves through the same four stages — tracked digitally at each handoff, so manufacturers know exactly what they're getting and hub operators get paid for what they collect.

Generated

Households, businesses & estates

Post-consumer plastics, aluminum, paper and e-waste set aside at the source instead of going straight to a dumpsite.

Collected

Community-based hubs

A network of local collection points, run by trained waste pickers, gathers and pre-sorts material by stream.

Verified

Digital tracking & sorting

Each batch is logged, weighed and graded, giving both hub operators and buyers a transparent record of volume and quality.

Supplied

Manufacturing plants

Sorted, verified feedstock is delivered to industrial buyers who need reliable recycled raw material.

Material streams

Four streams, four supply chains.

Each material has a different collection method, a different buyer, and a different reason it shouldn't be in a landfill. We run all four in parallel.

Plastics

PET, HDPE and PP collected from households and estates, baled by grade for reprocessing.

→ Packaging & resin plants

Aluminum

Cans and scrap sourced through hubs and informal collectors, cleaned and weighed for smelters.

→ Smelting & casting plants

Paper

Cardboard and office paper diverted from commercial waste streams before it's contaminated.

→ Pulp & paper mills

E-waste

Retired electronics safely dismantled to recover metals and prevent toxic leaching into soil.

→ Metal recovery facilities
Why it matters

One bridge, three problems solved at once.

1

Less to the dumpsite

Recyclable material that's formally collected and tracked is material that never reaches a landfill or an open burn site in the first place.

2

Real income for waste pickers

Formalizing collection turns informal waste-picking into a paid, structured livelihood — with youth and women as a deliberate focus.

3

Reliable feedstock for industry

Manufacturers get a consistent, verified supply of recycled raw material instead of chasing scattered informal sellers.
